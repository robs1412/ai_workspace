Standing context for Jewel tastings:

- Jewel demos require the TAB Demo Request form to be completed with proposed store numbers, dates, times, and product information including UPCs.
- Email the TAB Demo Request sheet to Jessica Victor and Mike Korkosz for store-level approvals.
- Use a three-week lead time before requested demo dates.
- Do not finalize the demo agency schedule until Jessica Victor and Mike Korkosz return approved stores.
- Jewel posts approved demos to its portal so stores are aware.
- The demo agency must contact the Liquor Manager at least 24 hours before the tasting to confirm approval with the store.
- Any product being sent into the store must be coordinated with the Liquor Manager one week before the tasting.
- These rules are compliance-critical; failure to follow the protocol can block live tastings for six months.
